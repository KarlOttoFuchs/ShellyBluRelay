The board slides the whole tube length without touching it

The script sweeps every one of the board's 34 parts along the full tube and tests it against the walls, then checks both caps. Nothing touches. The check is real: with 4 mm of headroom the connectors hit, and with no groove clearance the board edge hits.

Section A · tube middle, through the module (X = 0)

The board rides in a groove in each side wall

%%MID%%

The side walls are 2.8 mm so the groove leaves 1.8 mm of wall behind it. There are no rails or ribs. The board is held along both long edges for its whole length, with 1.5 mm of air underneath (no parts on the bottom). The antenna hangs over the 15.2 × 6.6 mm board notch, and the tube is PETG, so nothing metal is near it.

Detail · board edge in the groove (−Y side)

0.7 mm of overlap, 0.3 mm of play

%%GROOVE%%

The slot is the 1.6 mm board plus 0.3 mm. Printed standing on end, the slot height comes from XY accuracy and has no bridge to sag. FDM slots come out 0.1–0.2 mm tight, so print a short test section first.

Section B · IN end, through the connector and cap sleeve (X = −22.95)

The wires leave straight through the cable exit

%%CONN%%

The cap's sleeve slides 8 mm over the tube and snaps onto detents in the side walls. The push-in wire entries sit 6.7 mm above the tube floor, which is the height of the cable exit's centre, so the two cores run straight from the cable to the connector.

Section C · lengthwise, through one wire entry (Y = 2.0)

Two identical caps trap the tube

%%LONG%%

Each cap has a 12 mm cable chamber for stripping and fanning out the cores, a 2.5 mm end plate, and a screw tab flush with the mounting face. The tube butts against stop ribs inside each cap; the board has 0.2 mm of play at each end. The OUT cap is the same printed part turned 180°.

Top view · board in the tube, component side up

Pinhole and LED window line up with S1 and D3

%%TOP%%

The pinhole (Ø1.6) and the LED window (Ø3.2) are placed from the board file's S1 and D3 positions on every rebuild, so they follow any placement change. Dashed lines are the groove floors. The red strips are the 1.0 mm top-side keep-out the groove asks for along both long edges.
